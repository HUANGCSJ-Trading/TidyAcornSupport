Support 遊戲支援

Starting and saving

Tap the book cart to start or resume. The game saves your progress on your device. You can export or import a backup from the Pause menu.

Need a hand?

For gameplay, sound, or save issues, tell us your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the affected chapter if you know it.

Email Tidy Acorn support

開始遊戲與儲存進度

點選書車即可開始或繼續遊戲。遊戲會把進度儲存在你的裝置上。你可以在暫停選單匯出或匯入備份。

遇到問題？

如果操作、音效或存檔有問題，歡迎提供裝置型號、iOS 或 iPadOS 版本，以及遇到問題的關卡（如果知道）。

Privacy policy 隱私政策

Data in the app

Tidy Acorn does not collect or track personal data. It does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics. You can play offline.

Local progress and backups

Progress and settings are saved on your device. If you choose to export a backup, you decide where it is stored or shared. Tidy Acorn does not upload it to the developer. To remove local game data, delete the app. Delete any backups you exported separately from where you saved them.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we use the email address and details you choose to provide to reply and help with your request. You can use the same contact for privacy questions or to request deletion of your correspondence.

遊戲內的資料

Tidy Acorn 不收集或追蹤個人資料。遊戲不需要帳號，不含廣告或分析工具，並可離線遊玩。

本機進度與備份

進度與設定儲存在你的裝置上。如果選擇匯出備份，儲存或分享的位置由你決定，遊戲不會將備份上傳給開發者。刪除 App 即可移除裝置上的遊戲資料。已匯出的備份請到原儲存位置另外刪除。

來信聯絡

如果你寄信給客服，我們會使用你的電子郵件地址及你主動提供的內容來回覆及處理問題。若有隱私相關問題，或想要求刪除聯絡紀錄，也可以透過同一個支援管道聯繫。
